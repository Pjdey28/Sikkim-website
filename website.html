Culture of Sikkim

Sikkim is known for its diverse culture and traditions, with influences from Nepal, Bhutan, and Tibet. Festivals, music, and dance are integral to Sikkimese culture.

Festivals: Performed by the lamas one of the most fascinating types of musical dance, chaam. Maghe sankranti, saketa, chasok tangnam and bhimsen puja are popular traditional local festivals. Among the Buddhist festivals celebrated in Sikkim are losar , saga dawa, lhabab duechen, drupka teshi and bhumchu.

Traditional Dances and Music: The traditional dances like Mask Dance, performed during festivals, showcase the rich cultural heritage of Sikkim. The folk music, often played with instruments like the "Dramyen" and "Lingbu," is a significant part of Sikkimese culture.

Handicrafts: Sikkim is renowned for its exquisite handicrafts, including Thangka paintings, woolen carpets, and wood carvings, which reflect the artistic skills of the local artisans.

Cuisine: Sikkimese cuisine is a delightful blend of Nepalese, Tibetan, and Indian flavors. Popular dishes include Momos, Thukpa, and Phagshapa, often accompanied by fermented beverages like Chhang.
